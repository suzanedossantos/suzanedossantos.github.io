How I research

Understanding people

Semi-structured interviews, field research, participatory design and requirements gathering with experts.

Evaluating interfaces

Moderated usability tests, think-aloud, heuristic evaluation, cognitive walkthrough and the Communicability Evaluation Method.

Measuring experience

SUS, UEQ, SAM, PANAS and Likert scales, with descriptive analysis cross-checked against qualitative data.

Including those left out

Research with people with disabilities, older adults, trans and gender diverse people, and communities with little internet access, plus GenderMag and screen reader inspection.

About

I study how people evaluate, use and feel about technology. In my PhD, I am developing EmoFrame, a framework and system that supports planning HCI evaluations. Before that, in my master's, I brought emotional assessment instruments from paper to digital, in partnership with Psychology and Gerontology professionals.

My work covers accessibility, technology for older adults and gender diversity. I have also co-supervised undergraduate theses and taken part in outreach programs on digital education for older adults.
